Privacy Policy — Hau Para Fuma

Last updated:

Hau Para Fuma ("the app") is a smoking-cessation support app for Timor-Leste. This page explains, plainly, what information the app handles and why.

The short version

  • Your personal progress — quit date, check-ins, cravings logged, streaks, savings, settings — is stored only on your own device. It is never sent to our servers unless you choose to post it publicly in the Community feed.
  • We do not create accounts, do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and do not sell or share your data with any third party.
  • The only data that leaves your device is what you deliberately choose to share: a Community post, or (if you opt in) a push-notification subscription.

What is stored on your device

The app works offline and keeps everything about your personal journey — quit date, daily check-ins, cigarettes/money/health estimates, badges, and your settings — in your browser's local storage, on your own phone or computer. This data:

  • never leaves your device automatically;
  • is not visible to us, or to anyone else;
  • is removed if you clear the app's site data, uninstall it, or use the reset option inside the app's settings.

The Community feed

The app includes an optional public Community feed where people quitting smoking can post short messages of support to each other. If you choose to post or reply:

  • your message text and a nickname you choose yourself (never your real name unless you type it in) are sent to our server and stored so the post can be shown to other users;
  • posts are public to anyone using the app — do not include your phone number, address, or other personal details (the app blocks obvious attempts to do so, but please still be careful);
  • any post can be reported by another user; posts that receive enough reports are automatically hidden pending review;
  • if you want a post you made removed, contact us (see below) with enough detail to identify it — we do not require an account, so we rely on your description of the post and roughly when you made it.

Push notifications

If you opt in to reminders/notifications, your browser creates a push subscription (an anonymous delivery address, not tied to your name or phone number) which we store solely to deliver those notifications. You can turn this off at any time in your device or browser notification settings; disabling it removes the subscription.

Technical / server logs

Like virtually any web server, ours briefly sees the IP address of incoming requests, used only to prevent abuse (e.g. spam posting) via short-lived rate-limiting. IP addresses are not stored alongside posts, not linked to any profile, and not used for tracking.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • No analytics, behavioural tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Who this app is for

Hau Para Fuma is intended for adults who smoke and want support to quit. It is not directed at children. It offers general encouragement and locally relevant health information; it does not replace professional medical advice, and the app itself directs users toward local health services (e.g. Centro de Saúde Comunitária) for clinical concerns.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's own in-app privacy note.
